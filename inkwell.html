What It Does

Every tool a
screenwriter needs

Industry-Standard Formatting

Automatic slug lines, action, character cues, dialogue, and transitions — formatted perfectly as you type. No manual spacing. Ever.

Auto-Save

Every keystroke saved instantly. Never lose a word — even if the app closes unexpectedly.

Works Offline, Always

No Wi-Fi? No problem. Write on a plane, in the mountains, anywhere — completely disconnected.

Scene Navigator

Jump between scenes in a flash. Your entire screenplay always one tap away, beautifully organized.

Character Tracking

Keep track of every character in your script. Ink Well learns names as you write and auto-completes them, keeping your cast organized.

Export Ready

Export your finished screenplay as a PDF or plain text file, formatted and ready to send.

Distraction-Free Mode

Full-screen writing mode strips everything away. Just you and your story — nothing else.
